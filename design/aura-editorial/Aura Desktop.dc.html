Satin blouse, dusty rose
Dusty Rose · #C98D97
41 /100
Maybe — with care

Dusty rose is too cool and too light for a Deep Autumn. Worn near the face it flattens your depth and drains the warmth from your skin.

Tip — keep it away from the face: wear it as a skirt or lining, and put burnt brick or camel at the collar.
